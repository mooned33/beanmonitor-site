Auction intelligence

Cup of Excellence results, structured as data.

Auction pages are useful, but historical comparison becomes difficult when every result lives in prose or separate tables. BeanMonitor captures Cup of Excellence adjudications into a consistent dataset for green coffee buyers.

What BeanMonitor records
  • Program and category
  • Farm and lot identity
  • Variety and process when available
  • Cupping score
  • Final bid in USD/lb
  • Buyer identity when published

Why auction data matters

COE results are not a replacement for commercial differentials, but they reveal how buyers are valuing exceptional lots, varieties, processes and origins. A structured history makes it easier to compare new results with prior auctions instead of treating each headline as an isolated event.

Fresh results inside the daily brief

BeanMonitor checks public Alliance for Coffee Excellence result pages and flags newly detected adjudications. The most notable result can then be placed alongside price, origin weather and industry news in the same morning brief.

Source data originates from public auction result pages. BeanMonitor is independent and is not affiliated with the Alliance for Coffee Excellence.
