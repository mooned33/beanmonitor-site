Why coffee buyers should care

Weather matters differently depending on crop stage. Excess rain during drying can raise quality concerns, while prolonged dryness can affect development or yield. BeanMonitor does not turn a single weather anomaly into a buying recommendation. It makes the anomaly visible and places it beside price and market signals.

Regions currently monitored

Minas Gerais for Brazilian Arabica, Huila for Colombian Arabica, Sidama/Gedeo for Ethiopian Arabica and Dak Lak for Vietnamese Robusta. Monitoring can expand as the origin coverage grows.
