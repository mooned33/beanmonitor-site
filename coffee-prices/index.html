Why the 52-week position matters

A price can look expensive in isolation while still sitting near the middle of its recent range. BeanMonitor pairs the current quote with the 52-week high and low, then calculates a percentile. That gives green coffee buyers a quick reference point without pretending futures are the same thing as physical differentials.

Price is one signal, not the whole market

BeanMonitor reads price alongside origin weather, USD/BRL, Cup of Excellence auction results and industry news. The goal is not to forecast a single magic number. It is to surface changes that may matter for timing, availability and risk.
